# Chapter 3 — The Tide Archive

The archive smelled of salt and old paper.
Mira's eyes were green in the lamplight as she unrolled the chart.
Mira, who was 17 years old, had never seen the northern reefs.
She missed the mud flats; Mira lived in Saltmarsh until the flood.
Behind her, Orrin laughed and tapped the glass.
"You're reading it upside down," Tavi whispered from the doorway.
Tavi's sister Wren had drawn the chart forty years earlier.
